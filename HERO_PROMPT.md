# Olive Coffee House — Hero Image Prompt (for ChatGPT)

Attach these to ChatGPT together with the prompt:

1. **Layout reference** — `images/ref-hero-design.webp` (the "Drinko / ORDER COFFEE" design)
2. **Cup reference** — `images/cup-leaves.jpg` (dark green Olive paper cup in the leaves)
3. *(optional)* **Logo** — `images/logo-circle.png`, so it copies the badge correctly

When it's done, save the result as **`images/hero.jpg`** — the website picks it up automatically (no code change).

---

## 1. Clean description of the reference hero (what we're copying)

A wide, landscape product shot with a calm, minimal, high-end feel. One drink in a clear plastic cup stands dead-centre, sitting on a rough, sand-coloured concrete/stone block that acts as a pedestal; the top of the block sits in the lower third of the frame and its front edge runs out of the bottom of the image. Behind it is a plain, warm beige plaster wall with a soft hand-troweled texture — no objects, no props, nothing else in the scene. A wide, soft diagonal shadow falls across the wall from the top-right corner, as if late-afternoon sunlight is coming through a window off-frame, so the upper-right is darker and the centre/left is bright and warm. The lighting is natural and soft, with a gentle shadow from the cup onto the block. The colour palette is very narrow: beige, sand, cream, and the warm coffee tones of the drink. The cup is photographed straight-on at eye level, sharp, with a slightly shallow depth of field so the wall stays smooth. Large empty space on the left and right of the cup is deliberately kept clean so big headline text can be placed on either side of the drink.

## 2. The prompt (copy-paste this)

> Create a photorealistic, wide 16:9 hero image for a coffee-shop website, matching the composition, lighting and minimal mood of the first reference image (the "ORDER COFFEE" design) — but **without any text**.
>
> **Subject:** a single takeaway hot-coffee paper cup, exactly like the cup in the second reference image: matte **deep forest-green** cup (around #1F3D2B) with a **black plastic lid**, a cream-coloured circular badge logo printed on the front showing an olive tree with the words "Olive" on top and "Coffee House" around the bottom. Keep the logo accurate, centred and facing the camera. A tiny wisp of steam rising from the lid.
>
> **Placement:** the cup stands perfectly centred horizontally, on top of a rough, light sand-coloured **travertine / concrete block** used as a pedestal. The block's top surface sits in the lower third of the image and its front face runs off the bottom edge. The cup takes up roughly the middle 20% of the image width and about 55% of the image height.
>
> **Background:** a plain warm **cream / light-beige plaster wall** with a soft hand-troweled texture. Instead of a plain window shadow, the sunlight casts soft, out-of-focus **shadows of olive branches and leaves** diagonally across the wall from the top-right corner, so the upper-right is in gentle shade and the centre and left are bright and warm. A very subtle hint of sage-green bounce light in the shadows.
>
> **Lighting & camera:** natural late-afternoon sunlight from the upper right, soft and warm; gentle contact shadow from the cup on the block. Straight-on, eye-level product shot, 85mm lens look, shallow depth of field so the wall is smooth; the cup is tack-sharp.
>
> **Colour palette:** cream, warm beige, sand, deep olive/forest green, a little warm brown — calm, minimal, premium.
>
> **Very important:** keep the left third and right third of the image **clean and empty** (just wall and shadow) because big white headline text will be placed there. No text, no letters, no watermark anywhere except the logo on the cup. No other props, no plants in frame, no people, no table, no extra cups.

## 5. Tips if ChatGPT messes up the logo

- Say: *"The logo on the cup is wrong — redraw it exactly like the attached logo image: cream circle badge, olive tree in the middle, 'Olive' on top and 'Coffee House' curved along the bottom."*
- Or ask for the cup **without** a logo and we place the real logo on top in Photoshop/Photopea — usually the cleanest result.
